Laboratory Rules & Regulations
College of Computer Studies

To avoid embarrassment and maintain camaraderie with your friends and superiors at our laboratories, students must observe the following rules and regulations.

1. Silence and Discipline

Maintain silence, proper decorum, and discipline inside the laboratory. Mobile phones, walkmans and other personal pieces of equipment must be switched off.

Explanation: Students must avoid unnecessary actions that interrupt class. Devices can be on silent/vibrate for emergencies; calls must be taken outside.

2. No Games

Games are not allowed inside the lab. This includes computer-related games, card games and other games.

Explanation: Computers are for academic purposes only: research, keyboarding, and learning languages.

3. Internet Usage

Surfing the Internet is allowed only with permission. Downloading and installing software is prohibited.

Explanation: Social networking and non-educational sites are strictly forbidden.

4. Prohibited Content

Accessing pornographic or illicit sites is strictly prohibited.

Explanation: Violators will face a parent conference to discuss potential expulsion.

5. System Integrity

Deleting computer files and changing the set-up of the computer is a major offense.

6. Time Usage

Observe computer time usage carefully. A fifteen-minute allowance is given for each use before the unit is given to "sit-in" students.

7. Proper Decorum
  • Do not enter the lab unless the instructor is present.
  • All bags and knapsacks must be deposited at the counter.
  • Follow the assigned seating arrangement.
  • Close all software programs at the end of class.
  • Return all chairs to their proper places.
8. Prohibited Items

Chewing gum, eating, drinking, smoking, and vandalism are prohibited.

9. Conduct

Hostile behavior, yelling, swearing, or public displays of physical intimacy are not tolerated. Offensive individuals will be asked to leave.

10. Technical Issues

Report technical problems to the lab supervisor or instructor immediately.

DISCIPLINARY ACTION

First Offense: Suspension from classes recommended to the Guidance Center.

Subsequent Offenses: Recommendation for heavier sanctions.
